✦  Questions

Photo booth rental in Egypt, answered.

Tru Booth is a photo booth rental company based in Cairo. We bring an enclosed booth, in Black or Red, or an open booth for big groups, to weddings, engagements, parties and brand events across Cairo, Giza, the North Coast and El Gouna. Every booking includes a strip designed for your event, prints on the spot and an attendant from setup to teardown.

How much does it cost to rent a photo booth in Egypt?

It depends on the booth, the hours and prints you need, and where the event is. Send your date and venue on WhatsApp and we reply with a clear quote.

What does a booking include?

Up to 4 hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first, a custom strip design, an on-site attendant, and setup and teardown. Extra prints come in packs of 108.

How much space does the booth need?

About 2 × 2 metres, one power outlet and access before doors open. The booth runs fully offline, so the venue needs no Wi-Fi.

Where do you work?

Across Cairo and Giza, on the North Coast and in El Gouna, with other cities on request. You can also find our booths at Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed, where the closed booth The Black is open daily from 5 PM, and, in summer, at Seashell Hill Walk on the North Coast. From 22 November there are also Tru booths at The Drive 1 and The Drive 2 in New Cairo, and from 1 December at Park St, Americana Plaza, Bosco Hub and Arkan Plaza.

Can the photo strip be customised?

Yes. Every booking includes a strip designed for your event, with your names, date, colours or logo.
