Birthday photo booth rental for private parties in Cairo

Birthdays, Sweet 16s and house parties. Friends pile in, shoot a strip with the name and age on it, and leave with the print in hand.

Book NowSend an inquiry

In short

Tru Booth rents a birthday photo booth for private parties in Cairo, Giza, the North Coast and El Gouna: milestone birthdays, Sweet 16s and house parties at home or in a compound. Every strip prints on the spot with a custom design (the name and age, or the class year), and a Tru attendant runs the booth for the whole session. A standard booking covers up to four hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first. You book by WhatsApp or the inquiry form.

A party photo booth that sends everyone home with something

At a party, every phone is out and almost nothing ends up on paper. A photo booth turns the night into something people can hold: a strip with the name, the age and the date, printed within seconds.

It also gives the party a place to gather. Groups form around the booth, take turns and compare strips. The guest screen works in Arabic and English, so it is easy for everyone from grandparents to school friends.

Open or closed booth: which suits your party?

Both print your custom strip on the spot. The choice comes down to the crowd.

What a party booking includes

Your choice of booth
The enclosed booth in Black or Red, or the Open booth for big groups.
A strip designed for the party
The name, the age or class year, the date, your colours and a monogram or logo, on a three-frame or four-frame strip.
Prints within seconds
Up to four hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first, with extra prints in packs of 108.
An attendant for the whole session
Setup and testing before guests arrive, help at the booth throughout, and teardown at the end.
Video messages, if you want them
Friends record short, raw messages inside the booth, and every clip arrives uncut in a private folder within a week.

How much space does a party photo booth need?

A spot of about 2 × 2 metres where guests pass, room for a booth about 2 metres tall, one power outlet, and access before guests arrive so we can set up and test. No Wi-Fi needed.

Birthday and party photo booth questions

How much does it cost to rent a photo booth for a birthday in Cairo?

It depends on the booth, the hours and prints you need, and where the party is. Send us the date and location on WhatsApp and we reply with a clear quote.

Can the strip show the name and age?

Yes. Every booking includes a custom strip design: the name, the age or class year, the date and your colours.

Open or closed booth for a big group of friends?

The Open booth has no walls, so the whole group fits in one frame. The closed booth suits a private corner where friends squeeze in a few at a time.

Can you set up at home or in a compound?

Yes, as long as there is a spot of about 2 × 2 metres, one power outlet and access before guests arrive. The booth runs offline, so Wi-Fi does not matter.

How many prints do we get?

A standard booking includes up to 216 prints or four hours, whichever comes first. For a big guest list, add prints in packs of 108.

Do you do parties on the North Coast?

Yes. We work across Greater Cairo and Giza, on the North Coast and in El Gouna, and in other cities on request. Quotes outside Cairo include transport.
