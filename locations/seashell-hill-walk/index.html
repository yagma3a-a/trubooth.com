Photo booth at Seashell Hill Walk, North Coast

A Tru booth for the Sahel summer. Walk in with your friends, shoot a strip, and take the summer home on paper.

Book NowSend an inquiry

In short

Tru Booth runs a seasonal photo booth at Seashell Hill Walk on the North Coast (Sahel) during the summer. Anyone can walk in with friends, shoot a photo strip that prints on the spot within seconds, and pay per print at the booth, with no booking needed. For this season's dates and opening hours, message Tru on WhatsApp; to book a booth for a wedding, villa party or brand event in Sahel, use WhatsApp or the inquiry form.

A summer photo booth in Sahel

A Sahel summer is full of nights worth keeping, and most of them only ever live on a phone. The booth at Seashell Hill Walk is there for that: one strip, printed in seconds, to put on the fridge back in Cairo.

Walk up with your group, follow the screen in Arabic or English, and shoot your frames. The strip prints on the spot, and you pay per print at the booth.

Before you go

The booth is there for the summer season only. For this season's dates and opening hours, message us on WhatsApp before you head over.

The strip design at Seashell

At a residency the strip can carry the venue's name, and the frame can change with the season. Ask us on WhatsApp to see the current design.

Seashell Hill Walk photo booth questions

Is the Seashell booth open all year?

No, only for the summer season. Message us on WhatsApp for this season's dates and hours.

Do I need to book to use it?

No. Walk in, shoot a strip and pay per print at the booth.

Can you bring a photo booth to my villa or wedding in Sahel?

Yes. We cover weddings, villa parties and brand events on the North Coast. Send your date and location on WhatsApp; the quote includes transport.

What does the venue need for the booth?

A spot of about 2 × 2 metres, one power outlet and access before doors open. The booth runs fully offline, so no Wi-Fi is needed.
