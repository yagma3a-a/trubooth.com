Tru Booth locations and the areas we cover

Places where you can walk up to a Tru booth without booking, six of them new from 22 November and 1 December, and a service area that brings the booth to your own event across Cairo, the North Coast and El Gouna.

Book NowSend an inquiry

In short

You can use a Tru Booth photo booth without booking at Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed, home of The Black, Tru’s permanent closed booth, and at Seashell Hill Walk on the North Coast (Sahel), with a booth in summer. From 22 November there are also Tru booths at The Drive 1 and The Drive 2 in New Cairo, and from 1 December at Park St, Americana Plaza, Bosco Hub and Arkan Plaza. Anyone can walk in, shoot a strip and pay per print at the booth; Majarrah opens at 5 PM daily and closes at 11 PM Sunday to Thursday, midnight on Friday and Saturday. For weddings, parties and brand events, Tru brings a booth to you across Greater Cairo and Giza, the North Coast and El Gouna, and to other cities on request. Book on WhatsApp or through the inquiry form.

Where to find a Tru photo booth without booking

Tru Booth has residencies: places where the booth stays on site and anyone can use it. Walk in with your friends, shoot a strip, and it prints on the spot within seconds. You pay per print at the booth.

At Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed, our permanent booth is The Black, Tru’s closed booth, open Sunday to Thursday from 5 PM to 11 PM and Friday and Saturday from 5 PM to midnight. Our seasonal booth is at Seashell Hill Walk on the North Coast, there for the summer. To find the booth inside the mall, or to check that Seashell is running, message us on WhatsApp before you head over.

More walk-in booths from 22 November and 1 December

Six more places to walk into a Tru booth. For where each booth stands and its hours, message us on WhatsApp.

  • The Drive 1, New Cairo: from 22 November
  • The Drive 2, New Cairo: from 22 November
  • Park St: from 1 December
  • Americana Plaza: from 1 December
  • Bosco Hub: from 1 December
  • Arkan Plaza, Sheikh Zayed: from 1 December

Where we bring the booth

For weddings, engagements, birthdays and brand events, we bring the booth, the printer and an attendant to you. Where we work:

New Cairo and the rest of Cairo
Cairo is home, so weddings, engagements and company events anywhere in Greater Cairo are part of our regular service area. From 22 November you can also walk into our booths at The Drive 1 and The Drive 2 in New Cairo.
Sheikh Zayed and 6th of October
Our permanent booth lives at Majarrah Mall, from 1 December there is a Tru booth at Arkan Plaza too, and we also bring booths to events across Sheikh Zayed and 6th of October.
Giza
Giza is inside our regular service area, the same as Cairo.
North Coast (Sahel)
In summer you can walk up to our booth at Seashell Hill Walk, and we bring a booth to weddings, villa parties and brand events on the coast.
El Gouna
We travel to El Gouna for weddings and events, with transport included in the quote.
Other cities
Planning something somewhere else? Tell us the city, and we confirm whether we can be there.

Areas we cover

We bring the booth to events across Greater Cairo and beyond, including:

  • New Cairo and the Fifth Settlement, Madinaty, Rehab and Shorouk
  • Sheikh Zayed and 6th of October
  • Maadi, Zamalek, Heliopolis, Nasr City and Mokattam
  • Giza
  • The New Administrative Capital
  • The North Coast (Sahel), including El Alamein
  • El Gouna
  • Other cities, such as Alexandria, Ain Sokhna or Hurghada, on request: the quote includes transport

What the venue needs, wherever it is

A spot of about 2 × 2 metres (the booth is about 2 metres tall) where guests can see it and pass by, one power outlet, and access before doors open so we can set up and test. The booth runs fully offline, so a villa on the coast or a hall with weak signal is no problem.

How to book a photo booth outside Cairo

  1. Send the basics

    Message us on WhatsApp with your date, city, venue and the kind of event.

  2. Get a quote

    We confirm availability for your date and send a quote. For events outside Cairo, it includes transport.

  3. We arrive early

    On the day we set up and test the booth before doors open, and a Tru attendant runs it for the whole booking.

  4. Strips go home

    Guests take home strips printed in a design made for your event, and we pack everything away at the end.

Photo booth locations: common questions

Where can I use a Tru photo booth without booking an event?

At Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed, where The Black, our closed booth, is permanent (5 PM to 11 PM Sunday to Thursday, 5 PM to midnight Friday and Saturday), and at Seashell Hill Walk on the North Coast during the summer season. From 22 November there are also Tru booths at The Drive 1 and The Drive 2 in New Cairo, and from 1 December at Park St, Americana Plaza, Bosco Hub and Arkan Plaza. Walk in, shoot a strip and pay per print at the booth.

Is there a photo booth in Sheikh Zayed?

Yes. The Black, our closed booth, is permanent inside Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed and opens at 5 PM every day. From 1 December you can also walk into a Tru booth at Arkan Plaza. We also bring booths to events in Sheikh Zayed and 6th of October.

When can I walk into the new Tru booths?

At The Drive 1 and The Drive 2 in New Cairo from 22 November, and at Park St, Americana Plaza, Bosco Hub and Arkan Plaza from 1 December. For where each booth stands and its hours, message us on WhatsApp before you go.

Can you bring a photo booth to Sahel or El Gouna?

Yes. We cover weddings, villa parties and brand events on the North Coast and in El Gouna. Quotes for events outside Cairo include transport.

Is the Seashell Hill Walk booth open all year?

No, it is a seasonal booth for the summer. Message us on WhatsApp to check that it is running before you go.

Do you work in other cities?

Other cities are possible on request. Send us the city, date and venue, and we confirm whether we can be there and quote with transport included.

Does the booth need Wi-Fi at the venue?

No. The booth runs fully offline and needs only one power outlet.
