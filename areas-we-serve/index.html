Areas we serve: a photo booth from New Cairo to Sheikh Zayed, Sahel and El Gouna

Tru Booth is based in Cairo and brings the booth, the printer and an attendant to events across Greater Cairo and Giza, the North Coast and El Gouna. Find your area below.

In short

Tru Booth brings a photo booth to events across Greater Cairo and Giza, including New Cairo, Sheikh Zayed, 6th of October, the New Administrative Capital, Maadi, Heliopolis and Zamalek, as well as the North Coast (Sahel) and El Gouna. Other cities are possible on request. You can also walk in without booking at the permanent booth in Majarrah Mall, Sheikh Zayed, and in summer at Seashell Hill Walk on the North Coast. To book, send your date, area and venue on WhatsApp or through the inquiry form.

Where we bring the booth

Tru Booth is based in Cairo. Greater Cairo and Giza are our regular service area, and we also bring the booth to the North Coast (Sahel) and El Gouna.

Wherever the event is, the booking is the same: we arrive with the booth, the printer and an attendant before doors open, your guests get strips printed in your design, and we take everything away at the end. The booth runs fully offline, so a villa on the coast or a hall with a weak signal makes no difference.

Planning an event somewhere not listed on this page? Other cities are possible on request: send the city, the date and the venue on WhatsApp, and we confirm whether we can be there.

Walk in without booking: Sheikh Zayed and the North Coast

Two Tru booths are open to anyone, with no booking and no event needed: The Black, our closed booth, permanent inside Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed and open daily from 5 PM, and the seasonal booth at Seashell Hill Walk on the North Coast, which runs in summer only.

Walk in with friends, follow the screen in Arabic or English, and shoot your frames. Your strip prints on the spot, and payment is per print, at the booth. For where to find the booth in the mall, and this season’s dates and hours at Seashell, message us on WhatsApp before you head over.

What the venue needs, in any area

A visible spot where guests pass, about 2 × 2 metres for the enclosed booth (it stands about 2 metres tall), one power outlet, and access before doors open so we can set up and test. The open booth needs room in front for the group; send the venue and we confirm it. Nothing depends on Wi-Fi, since the booth works fully offline.

How to book a photo booth in your area

  1. Tell us where and when

    On WhatsApp or the inquiry form: the date, the area and venue, and what kind of event it is.

  2. We check the date and quote

    If the booth is free that day, we send a quote built for your event, with transport included outside Cairo.

  3. Setup before doors open

    A Tru attendant arrives ahead of your guests, sets up and tests the booth, then runs it until the booking ends.

  4. Teardown at the end

    Your guests keep strips printed in your design, and we pack the booth away when the booking ends.

Areas we serve: common questions

Do you come to New Cairo?

Yes. New Cairo, including the Fifth Settlement, Madinaty, Rehab and Shorouk, is part of Greater Cairo and inside our regular service area. Send your date and venue on WhatsApp for a quote.

Do you cover Sheikh Zayed and 6th of October?

Yes, for events at homes, compounds and venues across both. Sheikh Zayed is also where you can walk into The Black, our closed booth, inside Majarrah Mall.

Do you cover the New Administrative Capital?

Yes. Send the venue and the date on WhatsApp and we confirm availability for that day.

Do you work in Maadi, Heliopolis and Zamalek?

Yes. All three are inside Greater Cairo, our regular service area, along with Nasr City, Mokattam and Downtown.

Can you bring a photo booth to Sahel?

Yes. We cover weddings, villa parties and brand events on the North Coast, including El Alamein. In summer, you can also walk in at our booth at Seashell Hill Walk.

Do you work in El Gouna?

Yes. El Gouna is one of the places we travel to for weddings and events, and the quote includes transport.

What about Alexandria, Ain Sokhna or Hurghada?

Possible on request. Send the city, date and venue on WhatsApp; if we can be there, the quote includes transport.

Is there a walk-in booth in New Cairo?

Our walk-in booths are at Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed (The Black, our closed booth) and, in summer, at Seashell Hill Walk on the North Coast. In New Cairo, we bring the booth to your event.
