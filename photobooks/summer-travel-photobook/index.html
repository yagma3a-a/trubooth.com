In short

A Tru Photo Book turns a summer or a trip into a printed book from your own photos: a summer on the North Coast (Sahel), a week in El Gouna, Dahab or Sharm El Sheikh, or a trip abroad. It is an A4 hardcover (21 × 29.7 cm), case-bound, with 26 or 50 pages of 200 gsm matte paper, printed and bound in Cairo and delivered across Egypt or picked up at a Tru booth. You make it online at trubooth.com/photobook, approve the proof and pay in cash on delivery.

The summer, in one book instead of five phones

A family summer is photographed by everyone at once: the kids on the beach, the dinners, the sunsets, the road trip back. By September the photos are scattered across every phone in the family, and nobody sees most of them again.

A Tru Photo Book gathers them into one A4 hardcover, printed on 200 gsm matte paper, which keeps the glare down on bright beach photos.

Making a travel photobook

  1. Collect everyone’s photos

    One shared folder for the whole family, with original files rather than WhatsApp-forwarded copies.

  2. Tell it by day or by place

    A trip reads best day by day; a long summer reads well place by place or weekend by weekend.

  3. Mix wide views and close-ups

    Give a big landscape or sunset a full page, and group the small moments, the food, the details, four to a page.

  4. Choose 26 or 50 pages

    One trip often fits in 26 pages; a whole summer or a long trip is happier in 50.

  5. Make it in the shop

    Upload the photos and approve the proof. We print and bind the book in Cairo and deliver it to your door, or you collect it at a Tru booth, and you pay in cash when you receive it.

Tips for beach and travel photos in print

  • Bright beach photos print beautifully on matte paper; very dark night shots lose detail, so choose the clearest ones.
  • Straighten the horizon before you send a sea photo: on a full page a tilted sea is the first thing you notice.
  • Videos are not photos: if a moment only exists as a video, take a screenshot of the best frame only as a last resort, since it prints softer.
  • Leave out the twenty near-identical sunsets and keep the best one.
  • Write down where each set was taken; it helps keep the order of the book.

Travel photobook questions

Can I make a photobook from my phone photos from the beach?

Yes. Send the original files from the phones; bright, sharp photos print very well on matte paper.

How many pages for a summer photobook?

One trip often fits in 26 pages; a whole summer or a long trip suits 50. Extra pages can be added in blocks of four.

Can I combine photos from the whole family’s phones?

Yes. Gather them in one shared folder and send the originals; the book can mix everyone’s photos.

Do you deliver to the North Coast or the Red Sea?

Yes. The shop’s delivery list runs from Alexandria to Aswan and includes Matrouh (Sahel and the North Coast), the Red Sea (Hurghada and El Gouna) and South Sinai (Sharm and Dahab); you choose your governorate at checkout. You can also collect the book at a Tru booth.

What size is the travel photobook?

A4 hardcover, 21 × 29.7 cm, with 26 or 50 pages of 200 gsm matte paper, printed and bound in Cairo.
