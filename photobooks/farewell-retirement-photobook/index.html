A farewell gift: the group’s years together, in one book

For the friend moving abroad, the colleague who is leaving, the manager who is retiring.

In short

A Tru Photo Book makes a farewell or retirement gift from a group: the photos you share with a friend leaving Egypt, a colleague moving on or a manager retiring, in an A4 hardcover of 26 or 50 pages, printed and bound in Cairo. One person collects everyone’s photos, lays out the book online and approves the proof; the book is delivered to them anywhere in Egypt or collected at a Tru booth, and paid in cash when it arrives. At A4 it still fits in a carry-on bag.

A goodbye they can take with them

When someone leaves, for a job in the Gulf, a degree in Europe or a quiet retirement, the group usually gives a gift that could have come from anyone. A photobook can only come from you: the office, the trips, the late nights, the people they are leaving behind, in a book they will open in a new flat far from Cairo.

It is also the rare gift a whole group can make together. Everyone sends a few photos and a line, one person makes the book, and every page has someone in it.

Making one book from a whole group

  1. Choose an organiser

    One person collects, chooses and lays out the photos, so the book reads as one story, not a pile.

  2. Set a deadline

    Open a shared folder and give everyone a date to send their original photos by, a week or more before the goodbye.

  3. Ask for a line

    Each person sends one sentence for a caption under their photo: a memory, a joke, a thank you.

  4. Lay it out in the shop

    Upload the photos and the shop lays out the pages. Order them by year, or by person.

  5. Approve the proof

    Check every name and every caption before you approve: printing starts only after that.

  6. Collect the money, then the book

    The book is paid in cash when it arrives, so collect everyone’s share first.

Books for teams and companies

Tru Photo Books takes each book as its own order, paid in cash on delivery, and a second copy can be added at checkout. For a retirement or farewell book from a team, one person places the order. For more copies, or a company invoice, ask on WhatsApp before you order.

Farewell and retirement photobook questions

What is a good farewell gift for a friend moving abroad?

Something from home they can carry: a photobook of your years together in Egypt. Tru Photo Books prints A4 hardcover books from your own photos in Cairo; at A4, the book still fits in a carry-on bag.

How do we make one photobook from a whole group’s photos?

Choose one organiser, collect everyone’s original photos in a shared folder by a set date, and let the organiser choose and lay out the book in the shop. Ask for originals: photos forwarded on WhatsApp print soft.

Can the book be sent to our friend abroad?

No. Books are delivered within Egypt, to the person who ordered them, or collected at a Tru booth. Give it before they leave.

How do we split the cost of a group gift?

Collect everyone’s share before the book arrives: Tru Photo Books is paid in cash to the courier on delivery, or at the booth when you collect it.

Is a photobook a good retirement gift from colleagues?

Yes. A career in photos, from early team photos to the farewell party, with a line from each colleague, is a gift no shop can sell.

Can a company order several farewell books?

Each book is ordered and paid on its own in the shop, with an optional second copy. For more copies or a company invoice, ask on WhatsApp before ordering.
