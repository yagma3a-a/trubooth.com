In short

A Tru Photo Book is a personalised gift made from your own photos: an A4 hardcover of 26 or 50 pages that you lay out online at trubooth.com/photobook, approve as a proof, and receive printed and bound in Cairo. It suits Mother’s Day, an anniversary, a birthday, a new baby, a wedding, a graduation or a farewell. The book is delivered to your door anywhere in Egypt or collected at a Tru booth, and you pay in cash when it arrives, then hand it over yourself.

Why a photobook makes a good gift

Most gifts are chosen in a shop in ten minutes. A photobook is made from the life of the person you give it to: the trips, the family lunches, the years nobody thought to print. It is the gift that ends up on the shelf, not in a drawer.

It is also easy to make: upload the photos and the shop lays out the pages. Move any photo, change the order, add a caption; nothing is printed until you approve the proof.

Which photobook for which gift

Every book is an A4 hardcover. A 26-page book holds about 35 to 60 photos and a 50-page book about 70 to 120. A guide to the right one:

How to make a photobook gift

  1. Gather the photos

    Ask family or friends to put their original photos in one shared folder. Photograph old prints flat, in daylight, without flash, or scan them.

  2. Choose a cover

    Pick a ready-made cover or put your own photo on the front, and add a title, a line and a year, in English or in Arabic.

  3. Upload and lay out

    Upload the photos to the shop and it lays out the pages. Move any photo inside its frame, change the order, add captions.

  4. Approve the proof

    Check the names, the dates and how each photo is cropped, then approve with your full name. Printing starts only after that.

  5. Add the gift touches

    Add gift wrap: it comes with a card carrying the name of the person the book is for and your message.

  6. Receive it and give it

    It comes to you, or you collect it at a booth. Pay in cash, then hand it over.

The book comes to you, not to them

Tru Photo Books delivers a gift to the buyer, never to the person it is for. Have it sent where you will open the door, or collect it at a Tru booth, and give it yourself. You pay the courier in cash, so the bill never reaches them.

Making it personal

A cover with their name
Write their name, a title or a date on the cover, in Arabic or English typefaces, or use a photo of them on the front.
A caption from you
A line under a photo: the place, the year, a family joke. The shop warns you before you order if a caption sits too close to the edge.

Timing: gift days in Egypt

A printed book is not a same-day gift: the shop promises no delivery date, though a paid Rush option puts your book first in the print queue. Start early for these days:

  • Valentine’s Day: 14 February.
  • Mother’s Day in Egypt: 21 March.
  • Egypt’s own Love Day (عيد الحب المصري): 4 November.
  • Ramadan and the two Eids: about eleven days earlier every year (Ramadan 2027 is expected around 8 February).
  • Birthdays, anniversaries, graduations and farewells: as soon as you know the date.

Photobook gift questions

Is a photobook a good gift?

Yes, when the photos mean something to the person: it is made from their own life, and a hardcover book is kept and shown for years, long after flowers or chocolates are gone.

Can you deliver a gift photobook straight to the person it is for?

No. Tru Photo Books delivers a gift to the buyer, never to the person it is for, or the buyer collects it at a Tru booth. You hand it over yourself.

Can I add gift wrap and a message?

Yes. Gift wrap is an add-on at checkout and comes with a card: the name of the person the book is for and your message, up to 300 characters.

Where can I collect a photobook gift?

At a Tru booth you choose at checkout, such as Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed or Pulse Island in Cairo. You pay there in cash, and the shop tells you on WhatsApp when the book is ready.

How do I pay for a photobook gift?

In cash: to the courier when the book is delivered, or at the booth when you collect it. Nothing is charged in advance.

I need a gift soon. Can a photobook still arrive in time?

Possibly, but no day is promised: any delivery estimate the shop gives is an estimate, not a promise. A paid Rush option puts your book first in the print queue, and your private order page follows it until it reaches you.

Can the cover carry their name?

Yes. The cover can carry a title, a line under it and a year, in Arabic or English typefaces, on a ready-made cover or with your own photo on the front.

What if the gift book arrives damaged?

Tell Tru Photo Books within 30 days of delivery or collection, with your order reference and photos of the problem, and the book is reprinted or refunded, whichever you prefer.
