Graduation photobook: your university or school years in one book

Four years of lectures, friends, trips and the day you threw the cap. A Tru Photo Book keeps them together, in a hardcover you will open again in twenty years.

Start a graduation bookAsk on WhatsApp

In short

A Tru graduation photobook is made from your own photos of university or school and of graduation day. The Graduation edition is an A4 hardcover of 26 pages, about 35 to 60 photos, with the degree, the university and the year printed on the cover, printed and bound in Cairo; four full years fit better in 50 pages. Graduates make it for themselves, and parents, brothers, sisters and friends give it as a graduation gift. You upload the photos, approve the proof, and pay in cash on delivery anywhere in Egypt or at a Tru booth.

The years worth keeping, in print

University and school years are among the most photographed of any life, and among the least printed. The photos are spread across phones, group chats and old accounts, and the best of them disappear when a phone is lost.

A graduation photobook is the yearbook you make yourself: the friends, the place, the trips and the graduation day, in an A4 hardcover printed on 200 gsm matte paper in Cairo.

Three ways to make a graduation photobook

For yourself
Your own years, from the first week to the ceremony, in the order you lived them.
As a gift from the family
A graduation gift that gathers the years and the day, often with a few photos from childhood at the start.
For the group
Friends gather everyone’s photos into one book: the class book nobody else will make.

What to put in a graduation photobook

  • The first week: the first photos with the friends who stayed.
  • The place: the campus, the faculty, the café, the corner you always sat in.
  • The trips: the faculty trips, the summers, the weekends away.
  • The work: the project, the lab, the exhibition, the late nights before exams.
  • Graduation day: the gown and cap, the family, the friends and the photo booth strips if there was one.
  • A last page for the future: one photo of the group, the way you want to remember it.

Making a class photobook with friends

  1. Open one shared folder

    Everyone adds their original photos, sorted by year if possible.

  2. Choose one editor

    One person picks and orders the photos, so the book reads as one story.

  3. Choose 26 or 50 pages

    50 pages gives room for four years and a whole class; 26 suits a year or a small group.

  4. Make it in the shop

    Upload the photos, put the degree, university and year on the cover, and approve the proof. Each photo stays on one page, never split across two.

  5. Copies for the group

    A second copy can be added at checkout. For a copy for everyone in the class, ask on WhatsApp before you order.

Graduation photobook questions

What is a good graduation gift in Egypt?

A gift made from the graduate’s own years: a photobook of university or school and graduation day, with the degree and year on the cover. Tru Photo Books prints it in Cairo from your photos and delivers across Egypt, paid in cash on delivery.

Can I make a graduation photobook from my phone photos?

Yes. Upload the original files; photos forwarded through group chats are compressed and print softer, and the shop checks every photo before you order.

Can the cover show the degree, the university and the year?

Yes. The Graduation edition prints the degree, the university and the year on the cover.

Can our group of friends order several copies?

A second copy can be added at checkout. For more copies of the same class book, ask on WhatsApp before you order.

How many pages for four years of university?

50 pages gives room for four years; 26 pages suits one year or the graduation day alone. Extra pages can be added in blocks of four.

Can a graduation book be ready quickly?

The shop does not promise a delivery date, but the paid Rush option puts your book first in the print queue.
