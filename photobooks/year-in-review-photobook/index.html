A year-in-review photobook: your year, in one book

Twelve months of photos, one evening of choosing, and a book on the shelf before the year closes.

Make your year bookAsk on WhatsApp

In short

A Tru year-in-review photobook gathers a year of your own photos (the trips, the birthdays, the ordinary weekends) into an A4 hardcover of 26 or 50 pages, printed and bound in Cairo. It makes a New Year gift for 31 December, a Christmas gift for 25 December or 7 January, or a book a family makes for itself every year. You lay it out online, approve the proof, and pay in cash on delivery anywhere in Egypt or at a Tru booth.

Why a book of the year

A year leaves thousands of photos on a phone, and almost nobody scrolls back to January. A year-in-review book picks the best of every month and puts them in order: the trip in spring, the summer in Sahel, the birthday cake, the friend’s wedding, the quiet Friday that turned out to be the best photo of the year.

Made once a year, it becomes a tradition: the same cover colour with a new year printed on it, and in a few years a row of books on the shelf that tells the family’s story better than any phone.

How to make a year-in-review book

  1. Keep one album as the year goes

    Each month, add your favourite photos to one album on your phone. In December the book is half chosen.

  2. Pick three to five a month

    Thirty-five to sixty photos fill a 26-page book; seventy to a hundred and twenty fill fifty pages.

  3. Put them in order

    Month by month reads best. Give the moment of each season a full page.

  4. Upload and caption

    The shop lays out the pages. Add the month or a line under a photo, and print the year on the cover.

  5. Approve and receive

    Approve the proof; the book is printed and bound in Cairo, delivered to you or collected at a Tru booth, and paid in cash.

Which year book?

Every book is an A4 hardcover. A 26-page book holds about 35 to 60 photos and a 50-page book about 70 to 120.

Ideas that make a year book

A photo to open every month
Twelve opening pages, one per month, and the smaller moments around them.
January and December
The first and the last photo of the year on facing pages.
The year in a sentence
A caption on the first page: the trips, the new job, the new baby.
The people
One page for each person who made the year.

Start a yearly tradition

Choose one cover colour and print the year on it, then do the same next December. The cover carries a title, a line under it and a year, in Arabic or English typefaces, so every book in the row matches.

Dates to plan around

The shop promises no delivery date: any estimate it gives is an estimate, and a paid Rush option puts your book first in the print queue. Order in the first days of December for:

  • Christmas: 25 December, and 7 January, a public holiday in Egypt.
  • New Year’s Eve: 31 December.
  • A book for yourselves: any time in January, while the year is still fresh.

Year-in-review photobook questions

What is a year-in-review photobook?

A book of one year’s photos in order, month by month: the trips, the birthdays and the everyday moments, chosen from your phone and printed as an A4 hardcover.

What is a good New Year gift in Egypt for my wife or husband?

A book of your year together: the places you went and the days you want to keep, in order. Tru Photo Books prints A4 hardcover books from your own photos in Cairo and delivers across Egypt, paid in cash on delivery.

Is a photobook a good Christmas gift in Egypt?

Yes, for anyone with a year of photos worth keeping: parents, grandparents, a partner or friends. Plan for 25 December or for 7 January, Christmas for Egypt’s Copts.

How many photos for a year-in-review book?

About 35 to 60 photos for 26 pages, three to five a month, or about 70 to 120 for 50 pages.

Can the year go on the cover?

Yes. The cover carries a title, a line under it and a year, so a book each December makes a matching row on the shelf.

When should I order a year book for New Year’s Eve?

In the first days of December. The shop promises no delivery date, and a paid Rush option puts your book first in the print queue.
