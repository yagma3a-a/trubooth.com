A Mother’s Day gift in Egypt: a book of the family’s photos

Mother’s Day in Egypt is 21 March. Give her the photos she keeps asking you to send.

Make a book for MomAsk on WhatsApp

In short

For Mother’s Day in Egypt, on 21 March, a Tru Photo Book is a gift made from the family’s own photos: the old prints and the ones on everyone’s phones, in an A4 hardcover of 26 or 50 pages, printed and bound in Cairo. You lay it out online, approve the proof, and receive it at your door anywhere in Egypt or at a Tru booth, paying in cash when it arrives. It suits a mother, a mother-in-law, a grandmother, or both parents on their anniversary.

Why mothers keep this gift

Most mothers ask for photos and receive them one at a time on WhatsApp, compressed, between other messages. They scroll past them like everyone else. A book gives her the family in order: her own parents, her wedding, the children growing up, the grandchildren now, in pages she can turn with the people who visit.

It is also the gift that takes the most care to make, and she will see that. Choosing a hundred photos from forty years is an evening of work, often shared between brothers and sisters, and it shows.

What to put in a book for your mother

Her before you
Her childhood, her own parents, her engagement and wedding: the prints in the old family album, photographed or scanned.
The years she raised you
First days of school, summers, Eid mornings, the photos she took of you that you have never seen printed.
Her people
Her sisters and brothers, her friends, her neighbours: the faces she would want in the book.
Now
The grandchildren, last summer, the most recent family lunch. End the book in the present.
A line from you
A caption on the first page or under a photo: a date, a place, a sentence only your family would understand.

Gathering the photos without spoiling the surprise

  1. Make a shared folder

    One folder for brothers, sisters and cousins, with a date to send by. Ask for the original files, not photos forwarded on WhatsApp, which print soft.

  2. Borrow the old album

    Photograph each print flat, in daylight by a window, without flash, filling the frame with the photo. Then put the album back where it was.

  3. Choose and order

    Decade by decade works for a mother: her youth, the young family, the children grown, the grandchildren.

  4. Make the book

    Upload the photos to the shop, which lays out the pages. Put “Mama” or her name on the cover, in Arabic or English, and approve the proof.

  5. Collect it and give it

    The book is delivered to you, never to her, or you collect it at a Tru booth. You pay in cash when it arrives.

Getting it in time for 21 March

  • Start gathering in the first days of March: the photos from relatives are always the slowest part.
  • The shop does not promise a delivery date, so order early in March rather than in the last week.
  • A paid Rush option moves your book ahead in the queue if the date is close.
  • The book comes to you, so plan when you will hand it over: at the family lunch, or first thing in the morning.
  • If you collect it at a Tru booth, the shop tells you on WhatsApp when it is ready.

For a mother-in-law, a grandmother, or both parents

A book of her grandchildren is the gift a mother-in-law or a grandmother shows to everyone, so put “Teta” or her name on the cover. For both parents, a book of their marriage, from the wedding photos to the grandchildren, suits their anniversary as well as Mother’s Day.

Mother’s Day photobook questions

When is Mother’s Day in Egypt?

On 21 March, every year. Egypt has celebrated it since 1956, and much of the Arab world marks it on the same day.

What is a good Mother’s Day gift in Egypt?

A gift made from her own life: a photobook of the family’s photos, from old prints to the grandchildren, which she will keep and show for years. Tru Photo Books makes A4 hardcover books from your photos, printed in Cairo and paid in cash on delivery anywhere in Egypt.

How do I put my mother’s old printed photos in the book?

Photograph each print flat, in daylight, without flash, filling the frame, or scan it. Upload the file like any other photo; the shop checks every photo’s sharpness before you order.

Can my brothers and sisters make the book together?

Yes. Collect everyone’s original photos in one shared folder, then one person uploads them and lays out the book, so it reads as one story.

Can the cover say “Mama” in Arabic?

Yes. The cover can carry a title, a line under it and a year, set in Arabic or English typefaces, on a ready-made cover or with your own photo on the front.

What if I order too late for 21 March?

The shop does not promise a delivery date. A paid Rush option puts the book first in the print queue. If it still cannot arrive in time, show her the proof on your phone on the day and give her the book when it comes.
