A photobook made from your own photos

Most photos end up on a phone, scrolled past and almost never printed. A book is different. It sits on a shelf, comes out when family visits, and gets passed from hand to hand long after the phone is replaced.

Tru Photo Books is one well-made object: an A4 hardcover, printed and bound in Cairo on heavy 200 gsm matte paper. You bring the photos, from your phone, your camera or your photographer, and they become a book with big pages that give each photo room.

How to order a photobook

  1. Open the shop

    At trubooth.com/photobook, start the way that suits you: upload your photos, fill a ready-made edition, or upload your own A4 PDF.

  2. Choose the cover and the pages

    26 pages for about 35 to 60 photos, or 50 for about 70 to 120; extra pages come in blocks of four. Put your own photo, a title and a year on the cover.

  3. Upload your photos

    Upload the original files. Photos forwarded on WhatsApp are compressed and print soft; the shop checks every photo’s sharpness before you order.

  4. Change anything, then approve

    The shop lays out the pages. Move photos, change the order, add captions, then approve the proof. Nothing is printed before you approve.

  5. We print and bind it in Cairo

    Your book is printed on 200 gsm matte paper and case-bound in hardcover.

  6. Delivery or pickup, cash on arrival

    The book is delivered to your door across Egypt, or collected at a Tru booth such as Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed, and paid in cash when you receive it.

Getting your photos ready for print

  • Send the original files from your phone, camera or photographer, not screenshots or photos forwarded on WhatsApp.
  • Choose more photos than you need, then cut: a book with fewer, stronger photos reads better than a crowded one.
  • Put them in order: by time for a wedding or a trip, by person or by year for a gift.
  • Keep one photo you love for a full page: a portrait, a view or the moment the whole book is about.
  • Phone photos print well when they are sharp and well lit; very dark or zoomed-in shots lose detail on paper.

Photobook delivery across Egypt

Books are printed and bound in Cairo and delivered across Egypt: Greater Cairo and Giza, including New Cairo, Sheikh Zayed and 6th of October, Alexandria and the North Coast, the Delta cities such as Mansoura, Tanta and Zagazig, the Canal cities, the Red Sea from Hurghada and El Gouna to Sharm El Sheikh, and Upper Egypt. Photobook questions

What is a Tru photobook?

A printed book made from your own photos: an A4 hardcover (21 × 29.7 cm), case-bound, with 26 or 50 pages of 200 gsm matte paper, printed and bound in Cairo.

Can I make a photobook from my own photos?

Yes. Every Tru Photo Book is made from the customer’s own photos: from your phone, your camera or your photographer.

Is it only for weddings?

No. Couples order one after a wedding or katb ketab, families after a summer or a trip, and it also makes a gift for a parent, a couple or a graduate.

How many pages does a photobook have?

You choose 26 pages or 50 pages, and extra pages can be added in blocks of four.

How many photos can I put in it?

It depends on how many share a page. As a rule of thumb, a 26-page book holds about 35 to 60 photos in a comfortable mix, and a 50-page book about 70 to 120.

What paper do you print on?

200 gsm matte paper, with a hard case-bound cover.

Can I use photos from my phone?

Yes, as long as you send the original files. Photos forwarded on WhatsApp are compressed and can look soft in print.

Do you deliver photobooks outside Cairo?

Yes. Books are delivered across Egypt, or you can pick yours up at a Tru booth.

Can a photobook be sent straight to someone as a gift?

No. The shop delivers a gift to the buyer, never to the person it is for, so you hand it over yourself. Gift wrap and a card with your message can be added at checkout.

How long does a photobook take?

The shop does not promise a delivery date. A paid Rush option puts your book first in the print queue, and your private order page follows it until it reaches you or is ready to collect.

Do I see the book before it is printed?

Yes. The shop lays out the book, you change what you like, and you approve the proof with your full name. Printing starts only after you approve.

Can I pay cash on delivery for a photobook?

Yes. You pay in cash, to the courier when the book arrives or at the booth when you collect it. Nothing is charged in advance.

How do I order a photobook?

Online at trubooth.com/photobook: upload your photos, approve the proof, and pay in cash when the book is delivered or collected. Questions go to WhatsApp.
