Baby shower and gender reveal photo booth in Cairo

For the parties that welcome someone new: the shower before, the reveal in between, the sebou after. Family and friends step in, and the strip carries the baby’s name or the big question.

In short

Tru Booth rents a photo booth for baby showers, gender reveals, sebou celebrations and newborn welcome parties in Cairo, Giza, the North Coast and El Gouna, at home, in a garden or in a venue. Every strip prints on the spot with a custom design, from the baby’s name and the date to the colours of the reveal, and a Tru attendant runs the booth for the whole booking. The standard package runs up to four hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first. You book by WhatsApp or the inquiry form.

Why a photo booth suits a baby shower or a sebou

These are family parties. Grandparents, aunts, cousins and old friends gather in one house or one garden, and everyone wants a photo with the parents. A booth turns those photos into prints that guests carry home the same evening, with the baby’s name and the date on them.

Each print is a 4 × 6 inch sheet cut into two strips, so a guest can keep one and leave the other for the parents, or in a guestbook if they keep one. By the end of the party, the parents have a pile of strips from the people who came to welcome the baby.

Strip design ideas for showers, reveals and sebou

Your design can carry what you would put on an invitation. Some ideas:

  • Baby shower: the parents’ names, the date and a soft colour palette
  • Gender reveal: a “boy or girl?” design in both colours, ready before the big moment
  • Sebou: the baby’s name, the date of birth and the date of the sebou
  • Newborn welcome party: a welcome line and the baby’s name
  • A monogram of the baby’s initials
  • A bespoke concept with AI-rendered artwork, such as an illustrated nursery scene, on request

Booking the booth, step by step

  1. Tell us about the party

    On WhatsApp or the inquiry form: the date, home or venue and the area, whether it is a shower, a reveal or a sebou, and a rough guest count.

  2. Get a clear quote

    We confirm the date is free and send a quote. Outside Cairo, transport is included.

  3. Send names and colours

    We design the strip around them and you approve it before the party.

  4. On the day

    A Tru attendant sets up and tests before guests arrive, then runs the booth for the whole booking.

  5. After the party

    We pack everything away. Video messages, if you added them, follow within a week.

Hosting at home or in a garden?

Pick a spot of about 2 × 2 metres where guests pass, with room for a booth about 2 metres tall and one power outlet nearby, and give us access before guests arrive. No Wi-Fi is needed, since the booth runs offline. For a garden or a terrace, describe the space and we confirm whether it works.

Baby shower, gender reveal and sebou questions

Can you bring a photo booth to a sebou at home?

Yes. A sebou at home needs a spot of about 2 × 2 metres, one power outlet and access before guests arrive. No Wi-Fi is needed.

Can the strip carry the baby’s name?

Yes. Every booking includes a custom design with the name, the date, your colours or a monogram.

How does a photo booth fit into a gender reveal?

Guests use the booth before and after the reveal, and every session prints on the spot with your design. A design in both colours works well while the answer is still a secret.

Is the booth easy for older relatives?

Yes. The screen works in Arabic or English, and a Tru attendant stays at the booth for the whole booking to help.

Can family record messages for the baby?

Yes, with The Truth Room add-on. Guests record short messages inside the booth, and you receive every clip uncut in a private cloud folder within a week.

What does a photo booth for a baby shower cost?

There is no fixed price: each party is quoted on the booth, the hours and prints, and the location. Send the date and area on WhatsApp and we reply with a quote.
