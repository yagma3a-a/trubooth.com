Why a photo booth works on an exhibition stand

An exhibition hall is a long row of stands competing for the same few seconds of attention. A booth changes the question in a visitor’s head: not "what does this company sell?" but "shall we take a photo?". That is an easier yes, and it brings people onto your stand.

What they leave with matters too. A printed strip carries your logo and the visitor’s own face, which is why people keep it. It goes into the bag with the brochures and leaves the hall with them.

At a conference or a summit the rhythm is different: people sit through sessions and move in the breaks. A booth near the coffee area or the registration desk fills those breaks, and the strip carries the event name and date as a souvenir of the day.

What Tru brings to your stand

Your logo and message on every strip
Logo, brand colours and a one-line message on a three-frame or four-frame strip, in Arabic, English or both. On request, a bespoke concept design, which can use AI-rendered artwork.
In hand before they leave the stand
Each session prints within seconds on 4 × 6 inch photo paper, two strips to a sheet, so visitors walk on with the strip in hand.
An attendant on the booth
Before the hall opens, a Tru attendant sets up and tests the booth; during opening hours they guide visitors and keep the printer running, then pack away. Your stand team stays on the conversations.
No Wi-Fi, one outlet
A crowded hall’s Wi-Fi does not matter: the booth runs fully offline on one power outlet.
Two languages on screen
The guest screen works in Arabic and English, so international delegates and local visitors can follow it on their own.
Prints for a busy hall
A standard booking covers up to four hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first, and you can add packs of 108 when the hall is busy.

Strip design ideas for exhibitions and conferences

One clear idea per strip reads best. Ideas to adapt:

  • Your logo, the exhibition name and your stand number, so people can find you again.
  • The product you are showing at the fair, with one line about it.
  • A conference strip with the event name, the date and this edition’s theme.
  • A co-branded strip: your logo beside the organiser’s or a partner’s.
  • For a career or university fair, your logo with a short hiring line and where to apply.
  • Your Instagram handle or a campaign hashtag, so the strip leads back to you after the show.
  • An offer line, such as a code to show in your store, if you want to count redemptions later.

Turning the queue into conversations

The wait for the booth is the best moment to talk to people. They have chosen to stay on your stand, and they have a moment to listen. Brief your team to greet the queue, say what you do, and hand over a brochure or a sample while the group ahead finishes.

The booth itself gathers no data from visitors: there is no sign-up form, and nobody is asked for an email or a phone number. If you want leads, your team collects them its own way, with the visitor’s consent, away from the booth.

To look back on the stand afterwards, tell us before the event if you want the final print count and we confirm it, and follow the hashtag or handle you printed on the strip on your own channels.

How booking works

  1. Send the brief

    Your dates and opening hours, the venue and city, where your stand sits, how many visitors you expect at the stand, and your brand files, on WhatsApp or through the inquiry form.

  2. Get a quote

    We confirm the booth is free and send a clear quote. If the exhibition runs over several days, list every day and we confirm what we can do.

  3. Approve the strip

    We design the strip around your logo and message, and you approve it before the event.

  4. Setup before the hall opens

    We set up and test before visitors arrive, so check the organiser’s setup hours early.

  5. On the stand, then teardown

    Our attendant runs the booth while visitors shoot and take their strips, and packs everything away when the booking ends.

What your stand needs

For the closed booth, a spot of about 2 × 2 metres with room for a booth about 2 metres tall; for the open booth, room in front for the group, which we confirm for your stand. Either one needs a power outlet on the stand, access before the hall opens, and space in front for a short queue that does not block the aisle. No Wi-Fi needed. Exhibition venues may have their own conditions for outside equipment and power, so ask the organiser early and pass them on to us.

Exhibition and conference photo booth questions

Can the photo booth stand on our exhibition stand?

Yes, if the stand has room: about 2 × 2 metres and about 2 metres of height for the closed booth, one power outlet and access before the hall opens. For the open booth, send your stand plan and we confirm the space.

Does the booth collect visitor emails or leads?

No. The booth takes photos and prints strips, nothing more: no forms, no emails, no phone numbers. Any lead capture is up to your team, with the visitor’s consent.

Can the strip carry our logo and stand number?

Yes. Every booking includes a custom strip design with your logo, your colours and any short line you choose, such as your stand number or a hashtag.

How many prints does an exhibition stand need?

Count the visitors you expect to reach your stand, not everyone in the hall. A standard booking covers up to four hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first, with packs of 108 to add, and our prints guide has a calculator.

Can you cover an exhibition that runs for more than one day?

Send every date and the daily opening hours with your brief, and we confirm what is possible for those dates.

Do you work at exhibition venues outside Cairo?

Yes. Beyond Greater Cairo and Giza, we cover the North Coast and El Gouna, and other cities on request, with transport included in the quote.
