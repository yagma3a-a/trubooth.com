In short

Tru Booth rents a photo booth for Ramadan in Cairo, Giza, the North Coast and El Gouna: Ramadan tents, family and friends’ iftars and suhoors, company iftar and suhoor events, and Eid parties. Every strip prints on the spot with a custom design (a Ramadan or Eid theme, your names, or your company logo), and a Tru attendant runs the booth from setup to teardown. A standard booking covers up to four hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first, and the booth runs fully offline. Bookings are made by WhatsApp or through the inquiry form.

Why a photo booth works in Ramadan

Ramadan is the month of gatherings: iftar with the family, suhoor with friends, a company iftar with the whole team. People dress up, tables are long and the evenings run late, yet most photos stay on phones. A booth turns one of those nights into a strip guests can hold, with the date and a design made for the occasion.

After the meal, while tea and dessert go round, the booth gives guests a reason to get up and gather. Families step in together, colleagues who rarely meet outside the office share a frame, and everyone leaves with a print within seconds of their session.

Strip design ideas for Ramadan and Eid

Every booking includes a custom design. Ideas for the season:

  • Crescent and lantern (fanous) motifs with “Ramadan Kareem”
  • Patterns inspired by khayamiya, the colourful tent fabric of Ramadan
  • The family name and the date, for an iftar at home
  • Your company logo and brand colours for a corporate iftar or suhoor
  • “Eid Mubarak” with the family name, for the first day of Eid
  • A bespoke concept with AI-rendered artwork, such as a lantern-lit street in old Cairo, on request

How to book for Ramadan or Eid

  1. Send the night’s details

    The date, the start and end times of the iftar or suhoor, the venue and city, the type of event and a rough guest count. Booking more than one night? List every date in the same message.

  2. Availability and quote

    We check that the booth is free on every date you sent and reply with a clear quote. Outside Cairo, it includes transport.

  3. Approve the design

    Send your theme, names or logo. We turn them into a strip design for you to approve before the event.

  4. Ready before guests arrive

    We arrive ahead of iftar or suhoor, set up and test, and the attendant runs the booth for the whole booking.

  5. Teardown

    We pack everything away at the end. If you added video messages, the clips follow within a week.

Booking for a Ramadan tent or an outdoor iftar?

The booth needs a spot of about 2 × 2 metres where guests pass, room for a booth about 2 metres tall, one power outlet and access before guests arrive. It runs fully offline, so weak signal in a tent is no problem. For an open-air tent, a garden or a terrace, tell us about the venue and we confirm whether it works. Running a tent for the whole month? Ask about a seasonal booth on site.

Ramadan and Eid photo booth questions

Can you set up a photo booth in a Ramadan tent?

Yes. A tent works when there is a spot of about 2 × 2 metres, a power outlet and access before guests arrive. For an open-air tent, describe it and we confirm whether it works.

Can the strip have a Ramadan or Eid design?

Yes. Every booking includes a custom strip design, so it can carry a Ramadan or Eid theme, your names or logo, the date and your colours.

Do you do company iftars and suhoors?

Yes. Your logo and brand colours go on every strip, a custom backdrop is available on request, and a Tru attendant runs the booth so your team can host.

How long does the booth stay at an iftar or suhoor?

The standard booking runs for up to four hours or until 216 prints, whichever comes first. Tell us when your gathering starts and ends, so those hours cover the busiest part of the night.

Which booth suits a big Eid family gathering?

The Open booth: it has no walls, so the whole family fits in one frame. The closed booth suits smaller groups who want a private corner.

Can our venue keep a booth for the whole of Ramadan?

Ask us. Tru runs permanent and seasonal booths for malls and venues, on terms discussed on request. Message us with the venue and the dates, and we tell you what is possible.

How much is a photo booth for an iftar?

Each event is quoted on its own: the booth, the hours and prints, the location and the date. Send the details on WhatsApp for a clear quote.
