✦  Malls & Venues

Photo booth for malls and venues in Egypt

A booth that lives on your floor, puts your name on every print and gives visitors a reason to come back. We run it; you host it.

Book NowSend an inquiry

In short

Tru Booth runs a photo booth for malls, halls and destinations in Egypt, as a permanent installation or a seasonal residency. Tru staffs, stocks and maintains the booth, and your venue's name can sit on every strip. Terms are a revenue share or a fixed fee, discussed on request by WhatsApp or the inquiry form. Current residencies are Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed (permanent) and Seashell Hill Walk on the North Coast (seasonal, in summer).

A photo booth residency, with none of the work

Tru Booth places a booth in your mall, hall or destination and runs it: our team staffs it, keeps it stocked and maintains it. For the venue, it is zero effort. You give us a spot; visitors get a reason to stop, step in and take something home.

Visitors walk up, shoot a strip and pay per print. Every strip can carry your venue's name, so every print becomes an ad on someone's fridge.

What a photo booth for malls and venues brings

Your name on every strip
The strip design carries your venue's name and look. Every print leaves the building and ends up on a fridge or a mirror.
A reason to come back
Seasonal themes and changing frames (for summer, Ramadan or the holidays, for example) give regular visitors something new to shoot.
Zero effort for the venue
Tru staffs, stocks and maintains the booth. Your team does not run it, refill it or fix it.
A small footprint
About 2 × 2 metres and about 2 metres tall, on one power outlet. It runs fully offline, so the venue Wi-Fi never matters.
Two languages
The guest screen works in Arabic and English, so every visitor can use it.
Prints in seconds
Each strip prints on the spot on 4 × 6 inch photo paper, so visitors leave with it in hand.

How a venue photo booth partnership works

  1. Tell us about the space

    Send the venue, the spot you have in mind, and whether you want a permanent or a seasonal booth.

  2. Agree the terms

    A revenue share or a fixed fee, discussed on request.

  3. Design the strip

    We design the frame around your venue's name and look, with seasonal themes to follow.

  4. We install and run it

    Tru staffs, stocks and maintains the booth. Visitors walk up, shoot and pay per print.

What the venue provides

A spot of about 2 × 2 metres in a visible, high-traffic location, room for a booth about 2 metres tall, and one power outlet. That is all: no Wi-Fi, no staff, no supplies.

Mall and venue photo booth questions

How does a photo booth partnership with a mall work?

Tru Booth places a permanent or seasonal booth on site and staffs, stocks and maintains it. Terms are a revenue share or a fixed fee, discussed on request.

What does the venue need to provide?

A visible, high-traffic spot of about 2 × 2 metres and one power outlet. The venue does not need to provide Wi-Fi, staff or supplies.

How do visitors pay?

At our current residencies, walk-up visitors use the booth and pay per print.

Can the strips carry our venue's name?

Yes. The strip can carry your venue's name and look, and seasonal frames can change through the year.

Can the booth be seasonal only?

Yes. Seashell Hill Walk on the North Coast hosts a seasonal booth in summer, while Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed has a permanent one.

Which areas do you cover?

Greater Cairo and Giza, the North Coast and El Gouna, with other cities on request.
