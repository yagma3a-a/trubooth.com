One booth, many kinds of night

A photo booth does the same simple thing at every event: guests step in, shoot a few frames and leave with a printed strip in their hand within seconds. What changes from one occasion to the next is the design on the strip, the booth that suits the crowd, and where it stands.

Every Tru booking includes the same essentials: a custom strip design for your event, a Tru attendant on site from setup to teardown, and up to 4 hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first, with extra prints in packs of 108. The booth runs fully offline on one power outlet, so it works in a hall, a home, a garden or a mall.

How to choose a photo booth for your occasion

Four questions settle most bookings:

  1. How will guests come to the booth?

    In twos and threes, or in groups of ten? Couples and small groups point to the enclosed booth; big groups point to the open booth.

  2. What should the strip say?

    Names and the date, an age, a class year, a logo or a monogram. A custom design comes with every booking: a three-frame or four-frame strip, or a bespoke concept.

  3. Where will it stand?

    The enclosed booth needs about 2 × 2 metres and stands about 2 metres tall. The open booth needs room in front for the group, and whatever is behind them will be in every photo, so pick that wall with care.

  4. Which extras do you want?

    Video messages recorded inside the booth, a hardcover photobook after the event, and for brands, a custom backdrop on request.

What every occasion needs from the venue

A visible spot where guests pass, about 2 × 2 metres for the enclosed booth (about 2 metres tall), one power outlet, and access before doors open so we can set up and test. The open booth’s space depends on the group, so we confirm it for your venue. The booths run fully offline, so no Wi-Fi is needed.

Photo booths for events: common questions

Can I rent a photo booth for any kind of event?

Yes. Tru Booth brings its booths to weddings, engagements, birthdays, graduations, family celebrations, company events and brand activations, across Greater Cairo and Giza, the North Coast and El Gouna. Other cities are on request.

Which photo booth is best for a party?

For a close group of friends, the enclosed booth in Black or Red. For a big, loud party where everyone wants to be in one photo, the open booth. Tell us about the crowd and we suggest one.

Is the strip design different for each occasion?

Yes. Every booking includes a strip designed for your event: names, the date, an age or class year, your colours, a logo or a monogram.

What does a booking include?

Up to 4 hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first, a custom strip design, a Tru attendant on site, and setup and teardown. Extra prints come in packs of 108.

Can the booth be set up at home or outdoors?

At home, yes, with a spot of about 2 × 2 metres, one power outlet and access before guests arrive. Outdoors, it needs power, a visible spot and access before doors open; tell us about the venue and we confirm whether it works.

How much does a photo booth cost for an event?

It depends on the booth, the hours and prints you need, any extras, and where and when the event is. Send the date and venue on WhatsApp for a quote built for your event.

Can I try the booth before booking?

Yes. Walk into The Black, our closed booth at Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed, or in summer the booth at Seashell Hill Walk on the North Coast, and pay per print at the booth. From 22 November there are also Tru booths at The Drive 1 and The Drive 2 in New Cairo, and from 1 December at Park St, Americana Plaza, Bosco Hub and Arkan Plaza.
