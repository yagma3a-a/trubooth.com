✦  Engagements & Katb Ketab

Engagement and katb ketab photo booth in Cairo

For the smaller, closer nights before the wedding. Both families, one booth, and a printed strip with your two names and the date to take home.

Book NowSend an inquiry

In short

Tru Booth rents an engagement photo booth for engagement parties and katb ketab nights in Cairo, Giza, the North Coast and El Gouna, at home, in a venue or in a garden. Every strip prints on the spot with both your names and the date, and a Tru attendant runs the booth from setup to teardown. A standard booking covers up to four hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first. You book by WhatsApp or the inquiry form.

Why an engagement photo booth suits a smaller night

Engagements and katb ketab nights tend to be smaller than the wedding: close family and close friends, in a living room, a hall or a garden. The booth gives that kind of night one private corner where nobody is watching, so everybody is real.

Cousins squeeze in together, and the uncles who never pose take their turn. Every session ends with a printed strip in hand before the next song, with your names and the date on it.

A katb ketab photo booth for both families

At a katb ketab, the signing is the heart of the night and both families gather around it. Once the papers are signed, the booth is where the two sides step in together for their first photos as one family.

The guest screen works in Arabic and English, so every generation can follow it, and the attendant is there for anyone who wants a hand.

What an engagement booking includes

The booth
The enclosed booth in matte Black or Signature Red, about 2 × 2 metres and 2 metres tall, or the Open booth for big groups. Black and Red are the same booth on the same package.
Both names and the date
We design the strip around the two of you: your names, the date, your colours and a monogram if you have one. Every print of the night carries it.
Prints on the spot
Each session prints a four-frame strip within seconds. A booking covers up to four hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first, and you can add prints in packs of 108.
An attendant throughout the booking
A Tru attendant sets up and tests before guests arrive, guides people through the booth, keeps the printer running and packs everything away.
Video messages from both families
Add The Truth Room: guests record short, raw messages inside the booth, and you receive every clip uncut in a private folder within a week.

How the night goes

  1. Before guests arrive

    We arrive early, set up the booth and test the camera and printer, so it is ready before your first guest walks in.

  2. Guests step in

    The screen guides them in Arabic or English through a four-frame strip, which prints within seconds with your names and the date. Right after the signing or the rings is a good moment to send both families in together.

  3. After the last guest

    We pack everything away. If you added video messages, every clip follows within a week.

Hosting at home? What we need

A spot of about 2 × 2 metres where guests pass, room for a booth about 2 metres tall, one power outlet, and access before guests arrive so we can set up. No Wi-Fi needed.

Engagement and katb ketab photo booth questions

How much does an engagement photo booth cost in Cairo?

It depends on the booth, the hours and prints you need, and where the night is. Send us your date and location on WhatsApp and we reply with a clear quote.

Can a photo booth work at an engagement at home?

Yes. We need a spot of about 2 × 2 metres, one power outlet and access before guests arrive. The booth runs offline, so home Wi-Fi does not matter.

Can the strip have both our names and the date?

Yes. Every booking includes a custom strip design with your names, the date and your colours, plus a monogram if you have one.

Which booth suits a garden engagement?

The Red stands out among the greenery and is impossible to walk past, so it often suits a garden. The Black works just as well outdoors if you prefer something quieter.

Will both families fit in the booth?

The enclosed booth is made for family and friends squeezing in a few at a time. For one big photo of both families, the Open booth has no walls, so the whole group fits.

Do you cover engagements outside Cairo?

Yes. We work across Greater Cairo and Giza, on the North Coast and in El Gouna, and in other cities on request. Quotes outside Cairo include transport.
