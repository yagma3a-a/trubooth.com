Closed (enclosed) and open photo booths in Egypt

Two kinds of photo booth. The Closed booth is private and comes in Black or Red. The Open booth has no walls, so the whole group fits.

In short

Tru Booth offers photo booth rental in Egypt with two types of booth: the enclosed Closed booth, in black or red, and the Open booth, made for big groups. Both print a custom strip on the spot, with a Tru attendant on site throughout the booking. We cover Greater Cairo and Giza, the North Coast (Sahel) and El Gouna, with other cities on request. To book, send your date and venue on WhatsApp or through the inquiry form.

Types of photo booth: which one fits your event?

A closed (enclosed) booth is a private space guests step into. An open booth is a camera, a light and a screen with no walls around them, so a crowd can gather in front of it.

Choose the Closed booth for privacy and honest, unposed photos. Choose the Open booth when guests arrive in big groups and everyone wants to be in the same frame.

The Closed photo booth: The Black and The Red

Our enclosed photo booth comes in two finishes on the same package. Same booth, same prints: you choose the look.

The Black
Matte black, made for black-tie nights. Quiet, formal and at home in a classic hall.
The Red
Our signature red. Loud on purpose and impossible to walk past.
Private by design
The one corner of the night where nobody is watching, so everybody is real.
Size and power
About 2 × 2 metres and about 2 metres tall, running on one power outlet.

The Open photo booth: made for big groups

The Open booth is open-air: a pro camera, studio light, a touchscreen and a printer, with no walls around them. Nothing closes it in, so the bridal party, the whole team or a table of cousins fits in one shot, and the strip prints within seconds.

How much space and power does a photo booth need?

The Closed booth needs about 2 × 2 metres and stands about 2 metres tall. Either booth needs one power outlet, a visible spot where guests pass, and access before doors open to set up. No Wi-Fi is needed: the booths run fully offline. For the Open booth, send us your venue and we confirm the space.

How a photo booth session works

  1. We set up before doors open

    A Tru attendant sets up the booth and tests the camera and printer.

  2. Guests step in

    Into the Closed booth, or in front of the Open one. The screen guides them in Arabic or English.

  3. They shoot the strip

    Four frames, or three for a three-frame design.

  4. The print is in their hands

    Within seconds, with your design on it. When the booking ends, we pack everything away.

Closed or open booth: common questions

Which photo booth is right for my event?

Choose the Closed booth for privacy and candid photos, and the Open booth for big groups who want everyone in one shot. Not sure? Tell us about the event on WhatsApp and we suggest one.

What is the difference between The Black and The Red?

Only the finish. Both are the same enclosed booth on the same package: The Black is matte and made for black-tie nights, The Red is our signature red.

How much space does a photo booth need?

The Closed booth needs about 2 × 2 metres and stands about 2 metres tall. For the Open booth, send us your venue and we confirm the space.

Does the photo booth need Wi-Fi?

No. The booth runs fully offline, so a weak signal never stops it. It needs one power outlet.

Can the photo booth be set up outdoors?

It needs a power outlet, a visible spot and access before doors open. For a garden or a terrace, tell us about the venue and we confirm whether it works.

How many prints are included?

A standard booking covers up to four hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first. Extra prints can be added in packs of 108.

Where do you rent photo booths?

Greater Cairo and Giza (including New Cairo, Sheikh Zayed and 6th of October), the North Coast and El Gouna. Other cities are on request, and quotes outside Cairo include transport.
