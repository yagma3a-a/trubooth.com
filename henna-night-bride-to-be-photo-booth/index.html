✦  Henna nights & bride-to-be

Henna night and bride-to-be photo booth in Cairo

The nights before the wedding belong to the bride and her people. An enclosed booth gives them a corner of their own, and every strip carries her name.

Book NowSend an inquiry

In short

Tru Booth rents a photo booth for henna nights (laylet el henna), bride-to-be and bridal shower parties, bachelorette nights and girls’ nights in Cairo, Giza, the North Coast and El Gouna. The enclosed booth in The Black or The Red is private, so friends can be themselves, and every strip prints on the spot with a design made for the bride. A Tru attendant runs the booth from setup to teardown, and a standard booking covers up to four hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first. To book, message us on WhatsApp or send the inquiry form.

Why an enclosed booth suits a henna night

A henna night is close and loud at the same time: the bride, her sisters, cousins and best friends, music, dancing and fresh henna on everyone’s hands. Inside the enclosed booth, nobody is watching, so friends can be as silly or as glamorous as they like, away from the rest of the room.

Every session ends with a strip in hand within seconds, so the bride’s friends go home with the night in their bags and not only on their phones. Because every print carries her name and the date, the strips double as a small favour from the bride.

What Tru brings to the night

The booth
The enclosed booth in matte Black or Signature Red, about 2 × 2 metres and 2 metres tall, on the same package. Or the Open booth, if you want the whole group in one frame.
A strip made for the bride
Her name, the date, your colours and a monogram, on every print of the night. You approve the design before the day.
Instant prints
Strips print within seconds on a dye-sublimation photo printer. Up to four hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first, with extra packs of 108.
An attendant for the whole booking
Sets up and tests before guests arrive, guides each group through the screen in Arabic or English, keeps the printer running and packs away at the end.
Messages for the bride
Add The Truth Room: friends record short messages for her inside the booth, delivered uncut in a private folder within a week. Who else sees them is her choice.
No Wi-Fi, one socket
The booth runs fully offline on one power outlet, at home or in a venue.

Strip ideas for a henna night or bride-to-be party

The design is yours. A few starting points:

  • The bride’s name with “Bride to be” and the wedding date
  • Henna-inspired patterns framing each photo
  • The colours of the night, such as red and gold, or the shade of the bride’s dress
  • Both names and a countdown to the wedding day
  • A monogram of the couple’s initials that matches the wedding stationery
  • For a girls’ night: the group’s inside joke, a hashtag or every friend’s name
  • On request, a concept design built from an idea, which can use AI-rendered artwork

From first message to last strip

  1. Message us

    Send the date, home or venue and area, a rough guest count and the booth you like, on WhatsApp or the inquiry form.

  2. Confirm and quote

    We check the booth is free that night and reply with a clear quote.

  3. Design the strip

    Send the bride’s name, the date and your colours. We design, you approve.

  4. On the night

    The attendant arrives early, sets up and tests, and the booth is ready before the first guest.

  5. After the last song

    We pack away. Video messages, if added, reach the bride within a week.

Planning it at home?

Choose a spot of about 2 × 2 metres where guests pass, in the middle of the party rather than tucked away (a little back from the speakers if you add video messages), with room for a booth about 2 metres tall and one power outlet. We need access before guests arrive so we can set up and test. The booth runs offline, so no Wi-Fi is needed.

Henna night and bride-to-be questions

Is the booth private enough for a henna night?

Yes. The closed booth is enclosed, so only the friends inside are in the shot and nobody in the room is watching.

Can the strip say “Bride to be” with her name?

Yes. Every booking includes a custom design, so her name, the wedding date, your colours and a line such as “Bride to be” can all go on the strip.

Black or Red for a henna night?

Same booth, same package. The Red is loud on purpose and suits a bold, colourful night; The Black is matte and calmer, made for a classic room.

Can friends record messages for the bride?

Yes, with The Truth Room. Each friend records a short message inside the booth, and the bride receives every clip uncut in a private folder within a week.

Can you set up at home for a henna night?

Yes. A home works if there is a spot of about 2 × 2 metres where guests pass, one power outlet and access before guests arrive. The booth works offline.

Can we book the same booth for the wedding?

Yes, if it is free on both dates. Tru Booth also rents the enclosed booth for weddings: mention both dates when you message us, and we confirm availability and quote for each.

How much is a photo booth for a henna night?

It is quoted per event, from the booth, the hours and prints, and where the night is. Send the date and area on WhatsApp to get yours.
