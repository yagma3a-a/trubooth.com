Company family day photo booth in Egypt

Family days, team-building days, staff appreciation and sports days. Colleagues bring their families, step in together, and leave with a strip carrying your logo.

In short

Tru Booth brings a photo booth to company family days, team-building days, staff appreciation events and sports days, as well as school and club family days, in Greater Cairo and Giza, on the North Coast and in El Gouna, with other cities on request. Every strip prints on the spot with your logo, the event name and the date, and a Tru attendant runs the booth for the whole session, so HR and the organising team stay free. Bookings start with a WhatsApp message or the inquiry form.

Why a photo booth fits a family day

A family day is the one day colleagues meet each other’s families. The booth gives them an easy reason to stand together: a manager with their children, a team with partners and parents, a new hire meeting everyone at once. Each group leaves with its own print.

It works across ages. The guest screen guides each session in Arabic or English, the attendant is close by for anyone who has never used a booth, and the strip is in hand within seconds, which matters when children are waiting their turn.

The strip is also the part of the day that comes home. With your logo and the date on it, it ends up on a fridge or a desk, a small reminder of the day the company invited the family.

What Tru brings to a family day

Your logo and the day on every strip
The company logo, the event name and the date, in your colours, on a three-frame or four-frame strip.
Room for the whole family
The open booth has no walls, so parents, children and grandparents fit in one frame. The closed booth suits smaller groups and guests who prefer privacy.
An attendant, not HR
Our attendant is there from setup to teardown: testing before guests arrive, walking each family through the booth and keeping the printer going.
A strip for everyone
Each print is a 4 × 6 inch sheet with two strips, printed within seconds. For families of three or four, two copies per session would give everyone one; ask when you book and we confirm what is possible.
Video messages for the team
Add The Truth Room and colleagues and their families record short messages inside the booth, for a colleague who is leaving, the founders or the whole team. You receive every clip uncut, in a private cloud folder, within a week.
Offline, on one outlet
Club, compound or office, the Wi-Fi does not matter: the booth needs no connection and runs on a single power outlet.

Strip design ideas for family days and team events

Keep the company on the frame and the families in the photos:

  • Family day: the company logo, "Family Day" and the date, in brand colours.
  • Staff appreciation: a one-line thank-you, in Arabic, English or both.
  • Team-building: the team or department name and the challenge of the day.
  • Sports day: the tournament name and the date, so teams shoot their strip in kit.
  • School family day: the school name or crest and the school year.
  • Club family day: the club’s name and colours.
  • A company anniversary: the founding year and this year side by side.

How booking works

  1. Send the details

    The date and times, venue and city, how many staff and family members you expect, and your logo, on WhatsApp or through the inquiry form.

  2. Get a quote

    We confirm the booth is free and send a clear quote; for a venue outside Cairo, it includes transport.

  3. Approve the strip

    Send the logo and the name of the day; you see the strip design and approve it before the event.

  4. Setup before families arrive

    The booth is set up and the camera and printer tested before the first family reaches it.

  5. The day, then teardown

    Our attendant runs the booth for the whole session and packs everything away. If you added The Truth Room, the clips reach you within a week.

Space, power and outdoor venues

Family days often take place at a club, a compound or a garden. Wherever yours is, the booth needs one power outlet, a visible spot where families pass, and access before guests arrive. The closed booth takes about 2 × 2 metres and about 2 metres of height; the open booth needs room in front for the group, which we confirm for your venue. For a lawn or another open-air spot, tell us where the booth would stand and we confirm whether it works. No Wi-Fi needed.

Family day photo booth questions

Is a photo booth suitable for children at a family day?

Yes. The guest screen guides each session in Arabic or English and a Tru attendant helps anyone who needs it, so children can step in with their parents.

Can every family member take a strip home?

Each print carries two strips, so for a family of four, two copies per session would give everyone one. Ask for it when you book and we confirm what is possible.

Can the booth carry our company logo?

Yes. Your logo goes on every strip, with your colours, the name of the day and the date, as part of the custom design included in every booking.

Can the booth be set up outdoors at a club or compound?

It needs one power outlet, a visible spot and access before guests arrive. Tell us about the lawn or garden and we confirm whether it works.

How many prints does a family day need?

It depends on how many people attend and how many step in together. The standard booking includes up to 216 prints or four hours, whichever comes first, and extra packs of 108 can be added. To estimate it, use the calculator in our prints guide.

Do you bring the booth to team-building days outside Cairo?

Yes. Inside Greater Cairo and Giza it is our regular area, and team days on the North Coast, in El Gouna or in other cities on request are quoted with transport included.
