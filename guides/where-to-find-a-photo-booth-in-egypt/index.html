Where to find a closed photo booth in Egypt: walk in or hire one

Want to step into a closed booth this weekend, or bring one to your own event? Here is where to walk into Tru’s booths, when Majarrah Mall is open, and how hiring works.

By Aly Elghayesh · Published 25 October 2026

Book NowSend an inquiry

In short

In Egypt, you can walk into Tru Booth’s closed (enclosed) photo booth, The Black, at Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed: no booking, pay per print at the booth, open Sunday to Thursday from 5 PM to 11 PM and Friday and Saturday from 5 PM to midnight. To have a closed booth at your own event, hire one: Tru brings The Black or The Red, or the open booth for big groups, across Greater Cairo and Giza, the North Coast and El Gouna. In summer there is also a Tru booth at Seashell Hill Walk on the North Coast; for its dates, or the booth’s spot inside Majarrah Mall, message Tru on WhatsApp. From 22 November there are also Tru booths at The Drive 1 and The Drive 2 in New Cairo, and from 1 December at Park St, Americana Plaza, Bosco Hub and Arkan Plaza.

Where can I find a closed photo booth in Egypt?

Walk into one: The Black, Tru’s closed booth, is permanent inside Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed. There is no booking; you shoot a strip and pay for each print at the booth. It is open from 5 PM every day, until 11 PM Sunday to Thursday and until midnight on Friday and Saturday.

Or hire one for your event. Tru’s Closed booth comes in two finishes, The Black and The Red, and we bring it, with the printer and an attendant, to weddings, parties and brand events across Greater Cairo and Giza, the North Coast and El Gouna, with other cities on request.

In summer, there is also a Tru booth at Seashell Hill Walk on the North Coast (Sahel), where you can walk in the same way. From 22 November there are also Tru booths at The Drive 1 and The Drive 2 in New Cairo, and from 1 December at Park St, Americana Plaza, Bosco Hub and Arkan Plaza.

How a walk-in photo booth works

  1. Check on WhatsApp

    Ask us where the booth stands in the mall, or whether the Seashell booth is running this season.

  2. Walk in

    No booking. Bring whoever you want in the photo.

  3. Follow the screen

    It guides you through the session in Arabic or English.

  4. Shoot your frames

    Change the pose between frames; that is half the fun.

  5. Pay per print and take it home

    Your strip prints on the spot within seconds. Payment is per print, at the booth.

Closed or open booth: what is the difference?

A closed (enclosed) photo booth is a small private space with walls. Guests step inside, nobody is watching, and people relax in a way they rarely do in front of a full room. Tru’s comes in The Black, matte and made for black-tie nights, and The Red, loud on purpose. It needs about 2 × 2 metres and stands about 2 metres tall.

An open photo booth has no walls: a pro camera, a studio light, a touchscreen and a printer stand in the open, so a big group fits in one frame. Both print the same custom strip within seconds. For a point-by-point comparison, read the open vs enclosed guide linked at the end of this page.

How to hire a photo booth for your event

Send these details in one message on WhatsApp or through the inquiry form, and we reply with a quote.

  1. Date

    Which day, and roughly when the event starts and ends.

  2. Venue and city

    Where it is: the venue and the area, such as Maadi, 6th of October or Sahel.

  3. Event type

    What you are celebrating, from a katb ketab to a product launch.

  4. Guest count

    An estimate is fine; we use it to check whether a standard booking of 216 prints is enough.

  5. Booth preference

    Black, Red, open or undecided, plus any add-ons, such as video messages or a photobook.

Finding a photo booth in Egypt: common questions

Where can I find a closed photo booth in Egypt?

At Majarrah Mall in Sheikh Zayed you can walk into The Black, Tru’s closed booth, from 5 PM any day, with no booking; you pay per print. For your own event, hire Tru’s Closed booth in Black or Red, which we bring across Greater Cairo and Giza, the North Coast and El Gouna.

Do I need a booking for the Majarrah Mall booth?

No booking needed: walk in, shoot your strip and pay for each print right at the booth.

When is the Majarrah Mall booth open?

Sunday to Thursday, 5 PM to 11 PM; Friday and Saturday, 5 PM to midnight. To find where the booth is in the mall, ask us on WhatsApp.

Is there a photo booth on the North Coast?

Yes, in summer: our seasonal booth at Seashell Hill Walk. We also bring booths to weddings, villa parties and brand events on the coast.

How much is a print at a walk-in booth?

It is a per-print price, paid at the booth. Message us on WhatsApp and we tell you the current one.

Can I hire a closed photo booth for a party at home?

Yes, with a spot of about 2 × 2 metres, room for a booth about 2 metres tall, one power outlet and access before guests arrive. It runs offline, so Wi-Fi does not matter.

What is the difference between a closed and an open photo booth?

A closed booth has walls, so guests are in a private space; an open booth has none, so a big group fits in one frame. Both print a custom strip within seconds.
