Video guestbook in Egypt: The Truth Room

Inside the booth, your guests record a short message for you, in their own words. Every clip comes to you raw and uncut, in a private folder, within a week.

Book NowSend an inquiry

In short

The Truth Room is Tru Booth’s video guestbook in Egypt: during your event, guests record short video messages for you inside the booth. Every clip, raw and uncut, is delivered to you in a private cloud folder within a week. It is added to a Tru Booth booking in Cairo, on the North Coast or in El Gouna, and you can ask for it on WhatsApp or through the inquiry form.

What is a video guestbook?

A video guestbook swaps the book of signatures at the door for messages people say out loud. At a Tru Booth event, the booth doubles as a video message booth: a guest steps in, records a short message to you, and goes back to the party.

We call it The Truth Room, because that is what comes out when nobody is watching. The booth is the one corner of the night where guests stop performing for the room, so the messages sound like them.

How wedding video messages work

  1. Add it to your booking

    Ask for The Truth Room when you book, or message us on WhatsApp and we confirm it for your date.

  2. Guests record inside the booth

    During the event, guests step into the booth and record a short message for you.

  3. Nothing is cut

    We do not edit the messages. Every clip stays raw and uncut, exactly as it was recorded.

  4. Your folder arrives within a week

    Within a week, you receive every clip in a private cloud folder.

Why raw, uncut messages matter

An edited video decides for you which moments count. A raw one keeps all of it: the pause before someone finds the words, the friend who starts laughing halfway through, the aunt who talks to you as if nobody else is listening.

That is why we deliver every clip uncut. The stumbles and the half sentences are often the parts that sound most like the people who said them, and those are the parts you will want to hear again.

Are the video messages private?

Yes. Every clip is delivered to you in a private cloud folder. The folder is not public, and who else sees the messages is up to you.

Video guestbook questions

What is a video guestbook?

A guestbook made of short video messages instead of signatures. With The Truth Room, guests record their messages inside the Tru booth during your event.

When do we receive the video messages?

Within a week of the event. Every clip arrives in a private cloud folder.

Are the videos edited?

No. Every message is delivered raw and uncut, exactly as your guests recorded it.

Who can see our video messages?

The folder is private and is delivered to you. Who else watches the messages is your choice.

Can we add video messages to our booking?

Yes. The Truth Room can be added to a Tru Booth booking. Message us on WhatsApp with your date and we confirm.

Do guests still get their printed photo strips?

Yes. Video messages are added to a booking, so the booth still prints your custom strips on the spot.
