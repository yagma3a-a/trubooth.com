New Year and holiday party photo booth in Egypt

New Year’s Eve, Christmas, Halloween and the company year-end party. Dress up, pile in, and take the night home on a strip with the year printed on it.

In short

Tru Booth rents a photo booth for New Year’s Eve parties, Christmas parties, Halloween and themed parties, and company year-end parties in Greater Cairo and Giza, on the North Coast and in El Gouna, with other cities on request. Every strip prints on the spot in a design made for your party theme, with the date on it, and a Tru attendant runs the booth for the whole session. A standard booking covers up to four hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first. Book on WhatsApp or through the inquiry form.

Why a photo booth belongs at a holiday party

Holiday parties are made for dressing up. People put real effort into the outfit, the costume or the sequins, and then the night ends up scattered across everyone’s phones. The booth gives every outfit one proper photo, printed within seconds.

Put the year on the strip and it becomes the record of that night: who came, what they wore, who they stood next to.

At a company year-end party, the booth gives colleagues something to do together that is not a speech, and every strip leaves with the company logo and the year on it.

What Tru brings to the party

A strip in your theme
Gold for New Year’s Eve, red and green for Christmas, something darker for Halloween: we design the strip around your theme, colours and date, as a three-frame or four-frame strip.
A booth that suits the decor
The Red already looks like the season. The Black, matte and made for black-tie nights, suits a formal New Year’s Eve.
Prints in seconds
Each session prints on the spot, two strips to a 4 × 6 inch sheet. Up to four hours or 216 prints, whichever comes first, with packs of 108 for a big crowd.
An attendant for the whole session
Our attendant sets up and tests before the first guest, looks after the booth all night and clears it away at the end, so the hosts get to enjoy their own party.
Messages for the year ahead
Add The Truth Room and guests record short messages inside the booth, for the hosts or for the year ahead. The clips reach you raw and uncut, in a private folder, within a week.
No Wi-Fi needed
The booth runs fully offline on one power outlet, at home, in a villa or in a hall.

Strip design ideas for holiday parties

The strip is the souvenir, so let it say which night it was:

  • New Year’s Eve: the new year in big numbers, the date, and the host’s name or the venue.
  • A greeting for the year ahead, in Arabic, English or both.
  • Christmas: your colours, a monogram or the family name, and the date, for a family party at home.
  • Halloween: a darker palette and the party’s name, so the costumes are the star.
  • A themed party: the theme itself in the design, whether it is a decade, a film or a dress code.
  • Company year-end party: the logo, the year and a one-line thank-you to the team.
  • A bespoke concept with AI-rendered artwork, on request, for a theme that needs more than a frame.

How booking works

  1. Send the details

    The date, start and end times, venue and city, guest count and theme, on WhatsApp or through the inquiry form.

  2. Check the date

    Holiday dates are the same for everyone, so message us as soon as your venue is set and we confirm whether the booth is free.

  3. Approve the strip

    Tell us the theme; you get a strip design to approve before the party.

  4. Setup before guests arrive

    We set up the booth and test the camera and printer before the first guest walks in.

  5. The party, then teardown

    We run the booth through the party and pack it away at the end. If you added video messages, expect them within a week.

Practical notes before the night

The closed booth needs a spot of about 2 × 2 metres, about 2 metres of height and one power outlet; the open booth needs room in front for the group, which we confirm for your venue. We need access before guests arrive to set up and test. If the party is on a terrace or in a garden, tell us about the spot and we confirm whether it works. The booth runs fully offline, so the venue Wi-Fi never matters, however crowded the night gets.

Holiday party photo booth questions

Can you design a New Year’s Eve photo strip?

Yes. Every booking includes a custom strip design: the year, the date, your colours or theme, and a name or logo.

Does a photo booth work for Halloween costumes?

Yes. The closed booth gives small groups a private corner to show off their costumes, and the open booth fits a big group in matching costumes in one frame.

Which booth suits a Christmas party?

The Red matches the season and stands out in the room; The Black suits a formal, black-tie evening. Same closed booth, same package, different finish. For one big family or team photo, choose the open booth.

Can the booth carry our company branding at the year-end party?

Yes. The strip can carry your logo, your colours and a line for the year. On request we add a custom backdrop, and our attendant handles the booth while your team enjoys the night.

How many prints come with a holiday party booking?

Up to 216 prints or four hours, whichever comes first. A big party can add prints in packs of 108.

How early should we book for New Year’s Eve?

As soon as your venue is confirmed, since every party shares the same night. Message us on WhatsApp and we tell you whether the booth is free.
